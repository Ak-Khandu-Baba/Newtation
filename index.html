The Newtation GEO Audit Framework

An open method for auditing how ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot find, understand, trust and cite a brand. Version 1.0, September 2026.

Generative engines write one answer and name two or three sources. The page that ranks first on Google is often not one of them. The sources that get named are the ones a model can extract, attribute and verify. This framework measures that, dimension by dimension, and is published by Newtation, a Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) consultancy.

Six dimensions

#DimensionThe question
0AccessCan AI crawlers reach and render the page?
1EntityDoes the model know what you are, and can it verify that off-site?
2ExtractabilityDoes a claim survive being lifted off the page?
3StructureIs the page machine-verifiable, or only readable?
4CorroborationDoes anyone other than you say it?
5MeasurementAre you named, for which questions, on which engines?

Dimensions 0 to 4 score 20 points each for an AI Readiness score out of 100. Dimension 5 measures actual citations. Scoring, evidence levels and validation rules.
